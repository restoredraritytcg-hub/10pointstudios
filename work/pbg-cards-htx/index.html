Case study

PBG Cards & Collectibles HTX: one system for the website, the counter and order tracking

The client

PBG Cards & Collectibles HTX, a sports card and Pokémon shop at 25170 I-45 N, Spring, TX (as stated on their site).

pbgcardshtx.com →

The problem

A busy card shop needed its website, counter intake and customers’ order updates to work as one system.

What we built

The public website; a private order-status page customers reach from the QR code on their receipt; a staff portal for orders, grading stages, pickup clock and payouts, with a Website editor for hours, announcements, homepage, promos, events, shop photos and the Instagram grid; and an iPad intake app.

In the news

Community Impact covered PBG’s opening (“PBG Cards & Collectibles opens near The Woodlands”).
