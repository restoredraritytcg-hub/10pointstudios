FAQ

Is the free check really free?
Yes. The Free Website Check costs nothing and comes with no obligation. You get a 1-page PDF with your score and top 3 fixes, and it’s yours to keep.
What’s the difference between the free check and the True North Audit?
The free check is a quick snapshot: one score, your top 3 fixes. The True North Audit goes deep. It starts with how clear and intuitive your site is, covers content, performance, SEO, conversion and security, and benchmarks you against 3 local competitors. It ends with a prioritized action plan and a walkthrough call.
Why does the audit focus so much on clarity and UX?
Because that’s where most sites lose people. If visitors can’t quickly tell what you do, who it’s for and what to do next, nothing else matters much. So it’s the first thing we look at.
Will the security review affect my website?
It’s designed not to. The review is passive and non-invasive: we only look at what your site already shows to any visitor, such as its SSL certificate, platform versions and security headers. We don’t log in, submit forms, scan ports, or attempt to break in, and the True North review happens only with your written authorization. It isn’t a penetration test or a full security audit, so it can’t guarantee your site is secure. It’s a check of the basics with clear next steps.
How do you choose the competitors?
Together. We agree on 3 local competitors with you, so the comparison reflects who your customers are actually choosing between.
How does the credit work?
The True North Audit is a one-time fee. If you sign a 10Point Studios website or app build within 60 days of audit delivery, the full fee is credited toward the total cost of your project.
Do I have to build with you afterward?
No. Your report and action plan are yours to keep and act on however you choose.
