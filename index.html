What we can build

Already running, ready to reuse.

These are capabilities already running on sites and apps we have built and still manage. If you can describe the job, we can almost certainly start from something that exists.

01

Service intake that runs the shop

Multi-item request forms with photos, service choices, and hand-off options. Customers start a job online; you confirm the work before anything is shipped.

02

Quote calculators you control

Live totals from service tiers, add-ons, and referral codes. The number is a starting point you can still confirm with the customer.

03

Where is my order? pages

Customers look up a reference number or scan a receipt and see the stage, notes, and next step without calling the shop.

04

Scan-powered listing and intake tools

A crooked phone photo or scanner page comes out straightened, measured for centering, and cut into the close-ups a listing or a workbench needs.

05

Counter apps and staff portals

An installable iPad check-in, a staff dashboard for orders and payouts, and a private admin for photos, notes, and status.

06

Shop sites that stay current

Hours, open-now status, maps, promotions, and homepage photos you can update yourself, plus the local SEO a storefront needs.

07

Accounts, plans, and installable web apps

Sign-in, subscription plans, and a home-screen app so a tool feels like a product, not a brochure.

08

Galleries, shows, and reviews

Before-and-after work, upcoming event calendars, and a review form that only goes live after you approve it.

Process

Five points on the compass.

A straight path from the first conversation to a product we keep in shape.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We listen, look at what you have, and agree what “a 10” means for this project.

  2. 02

    Design

    Structure, interface, and a visual system tight enough to build from.

  3. 03

    Build

    The site or app, produced carefully and checked before anyone else sees it.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Go live with hosting, domain, and a handoff that isn’t a dump of files.

  5. 05

    Guide

    Managed maintenance after launch: updates, care, and a single place to ask.
