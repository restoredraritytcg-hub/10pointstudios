Who builds it

10Point Studios is led by Scott Shipley. Before starting the studio, Scott built apps for Meta, Disney and Under Armour, and ran major projects for Standard Chartered Bank, Nike and Blue Cross Blue Shield. That work taught him what enterprise-grade delivery takes: clear scope, careful builds and launches that hold up. 10Point grew out of Restored Rarity TCG, the card-restoration business Scott built and still runs, and brings that same standard to every client. You get big-company discipline from a studio small enough that you work directly with the person building it.
