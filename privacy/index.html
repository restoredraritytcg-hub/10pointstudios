Privacy

How we use the information you send us through this website.

Last updated: 27 November 2026. This wording should be reviewed by counsel (Leroy) before it is treated as final.

Who we are

10Point Studios is Scott Shipley's web and app studio, based in Conroe, Texas, and serving Conroe, The Woodlands and Houston. Email: 10pointstudios@protonmail.com.

What we collect

This website collects information only when you fill in a form:

  • Start a project (contact form): your name, email address, what you need, and a short message about the project, plus the authorization checkbox.
  • Free Website Check form: your name, email address, website address, a short note about your business, and the authorization checkbox.

A hidden “company website” field on those forms is a honeypot to catch spam and is not used as a real question.

How that information is handled

  • Both forms are sent through FormSubmit to the studio inbox at 10pointstudios@protonmail.com. A copy is also sent to restoredraritytcg@gmail.com.
  • We use that information to reply to your inquiry, usually within one business day, and to follow up about a project or a Website Check.
  • This site does not currently include a visitor-analytics or advertising pixel. If that changes, this page should be updated.
  • Fonts and images for this site are hosted with the site itself.

We do not sell this information. How long form messages are kept, and any other tools later added to the site, should be reflected here after review.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to ask us to update or delete it, email 10pointstudios@protonmail.com.
